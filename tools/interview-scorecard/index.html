Interview Scorecard

Score every candidate on the same criteria, then compare fairly.

Works offline - candidate data never leaves this device

Criterion Weight Remove

Weights are relative: 30 counts twice as much as 15. They do not have to add up to 100.

2. Score a candidate

Scale: 1 Weak, 2 Below expectations, 3 Meets expectations, 4 Strong, 5 Outstanding. Score on evidence from the interview, not on gut feeling.

3. Ranking

Rank Candidate Score (of 5) Percent Recommendation Rated Interviewers disagree on

Add candidates and scores to see the ranking.

The ranking supports a decision; it does not make it. Review disagreements and notes together, and keep records according to your company policy.
