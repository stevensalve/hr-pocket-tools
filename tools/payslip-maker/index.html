2. Salary data

One row per employee. Needed: Name and at least one earning (Basic, HRA, ...). Deductions are recognised by name (PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS, Loan...). You can also prefix a header with E: or D: to force earning or deduction. Gross and Net columns are ignored; the tool adds everything up.

Payslips are not password-protected. Send each one only to its owner, over a channel your company approves, and delete downloaded files when finished. Check totals against your payroll records before sending.
