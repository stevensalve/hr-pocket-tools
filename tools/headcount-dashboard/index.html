How are these numbers worked out?
  • Headcount on a date counts people who joined by then and whose last working day is after it.
  • Attrition % = leavers in the period divided by average headcount (opening plus closing, divided by two), times 100.
  • Annualised scales the period figure to a 365-day year, so a quarter can be compared with a year.
  • First-year share is the part of leavers who left within a year of joining.
